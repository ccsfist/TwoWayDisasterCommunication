# Disaster Preparation Risk Communications are Challenging

## Coordination is hard during disasters

- Leadership plays a central role
- Communities/families/individuals make their decisions and take action

- Rapid and trusted communication is the most important factor 
- Tribal Nations: 
	- Historical reasons for mistrust of government risk comm
	- Language translation challenges
	- Lack of incorporation of Tribal Council viewpoints 

- Standard strategy of sending one way blanket alerts has been useful but has many limits

- Town halls and surveys have value, but also leave communication gaps

- Modern technology allows for two way phone messaging
	- But we need new methods to take advantage of that possibility

- Lets roleplay to explore how to use the technology

Imagine you are expecting heavy floods on a reservation and there are people living in a flood-prone area. Before the flood hits, it’s your job to make sure that they evacuate to a shelter, or can shelter in place.

Shelter managers have the complex questions of
